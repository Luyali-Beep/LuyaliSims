About LuyaliSims

LuyaliSims is a dedicated open-access educational repository offering browser-based scientific simulations. By transforming abstract mathematical data and physical laws into interactive 3D tools, we make STEM learning experiential, verifiable, and globally accessible.

🚀 Content updated weekly. Built to assist secondary and collegiate STEM curricula worldwide.

About the Author

Ian Luyali is an educator, EdTech developer, and creative strategist. Through Click & Draft, he develops digital instructional media, curriculum tools, and interactive browser laboratories designed to lower the barrier to science comprehension.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to LuyaliSims. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we treat information when you interact with our educational simulators.

1. Educational Purpose & Information Collection

LuyaliSims is a free educational portal. We do not require account registration, passwords, or personal login information to run any simulation. All simulation calculations (e.g., mass, voltage, deflection) occur locally within your browser using client-side JavaScript.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense to fund our free educational tools. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site based on users' visits to this and other sites on the Internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to LuyaliSims and other sites.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. Log Files & Analytics

Like standard websites, our hosting servers collect anonymous log records (including IP addresses, browser types, and timestamp references) to monitor server uptime, fix script errors, and optimize simulator performance.

4. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our educational simulators are built for students and educators of all ages. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions regarding this policy, contact us at: luyashliz@gmail.com.
